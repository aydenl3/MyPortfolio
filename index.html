About

I'm a game designer who likes to create places to explore creative decision-making. I got my Bachelor's Degree at UC Santa Cruz. I love incentivizing players to make odd decisions, and be able to express their creativity. I enjoy designing in groups, and communicating with others to pursue a collective vision. My favorite game right now is Boomerang Fu! Recently I've been getting into Rock Climbing!
